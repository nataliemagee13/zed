# Reflection

## The Technique I Learned

I learned how the Fisher-Yates shuffle creates a randomized copy of an array by moving backward through the list and swapping each item with a randomly selected earlier item. The app uses the shuffled list to recommend songs without repeating them until the available songs for that mood have been used.

## Judgment Calls AI Couldn't Make

I chose the visual direction for the app: a soft 1989-inspired coastal palette with friendship-bracelet details, mood-based colors, and a readable result card. I also decided that the interface should stay playful without letting decorations interfere with the song recommendation, favorites, or keyboard accessibility.
